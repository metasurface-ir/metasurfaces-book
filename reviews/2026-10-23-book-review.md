# English book review and Chapter 6 release

## Scope

Reviewed the developed English Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10, their worked
guides, review pages, labs, notebook narratives/code, numerical conventions,
visual assets and orientation pages. Chapters 7–9 remain syllabus outlines.
The resulting scientific/editorial changes have matching Persian content.

## Findings and changes

- **Chapter 1:** analytical two-port assumptions, phase/delay signs, limits and
  power normalization were consistent. Keep its existing original plots and
  worked examples; no additional conceptual diagram was needed here.
- **Chapter 2:** add the missing computed perturbation/retrieval sensitivity
  evidence requested by the lab. Distinguish model-consistent perturbations
  from independent noise that violates electric-sheet continuity. Preserve
  the common tangential-electric Fresnel basis and face phase references.
- **Chapter 3:** retain the checked TE solver/library and add a target phase ray,
  transmission-threshold circle and candidate eligibility highlighting to the
  offline complex-response explorer. Its controls continue to select saved data.
- **Chapter 4:** allowed-order, cutoff, scalar-coefficient and flux-normalization
  explanations were consistent. Keep the existing explorer and original figures.
- **Chapter 5:** correct notebook parameters that previously diverged from
  default geometry used in some solves; guard saved-library compatibility.
  Independently refine scalar window/grid for continuous, sampled-phase and
  local-library apertures with common normalization and fixed integration bounds.
  Preserve scalar/Jones versus full-wave distinctions and all open channels.
- **Chapter 10:** add an original complex-transmission passivity disk/circle
  diagnostic for the restricted equal-exterior electric-shunt model. Explain
  interpolation's implied artificial loss and distinguish passive predictions
  from the exact lossless response. Keep synthetic-data claims restricted.
- **Chapter 6:** add a parameterized original FDFD slab, an independent exact
  Fresnel reference, mesh/field/boundary/deembedding diagnostics, RCWA harmonic
  companion, worked examples, self-check/rubric, lab, executed notebook, complete
  source ZIP, and fully bilingual offline explorer. Four new original figures
  show actual calculations. FEM/FDTD and finite-device extensions are outlines.
- **Sources:** add bilingual provenance/reuse guidance and an image inventory
  checked against saved notebook bytes. No publisher figures or paper
  screenshots were introduced; no general reuse license was assigned.

## Validation policy

Execute changed notebooks from fresh kernels, rerun relevant analytical/full-wave
audits, export original plot bytes, verify bilingual equations/code/source locks,
strict-build both editions, and inspect the changed pages, figures, download
bundles and explorer controls in a browser before publication. The numerical
audit records its actual tolerances, cases, versions, source hashes and scope.
Computational verification is not experimental validation or copyright clearance.
