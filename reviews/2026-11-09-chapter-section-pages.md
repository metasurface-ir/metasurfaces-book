# Chapter 1 and 2 section pages

Each numbered section is now its own page beneath its chapter in both sidebars: eleven Chapter 1 sections and sixteen Chapter 2 sections. Smaller subsections remain inside their numbered section. The existing chapter routes retain their opening text and link to the section pages. Companion guides, reviews, notebooks, and browser labs retain their routes.

This is a structural move. Reconstructing all section bodies, reversing only the section-title heading level, reproduces the preceding full chapter files exactly in both languages. `translations/chapter-sections.json` records the migration hashes and page map. These hashes record this migration, not a requirement to freeze later lesson edits. Source reading coverage now spans the corresponding section pages. Previously shared explicit Chapter 2 section-anchor URLs redirect to their new section page; language switching pairs the new routes.

Validation includes translation/source locks, unchanged scientific notebook identities, strict bilingual build, and browser checks of section navigation, equations, exercise toggles, language switching, and narrow-screen layout.
