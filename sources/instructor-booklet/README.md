# Instructor-supplied primary reading

`chapter01-fa.md` is a manual transcription of Chapter 1, printed pages 5–14, of the instructor-supplied *کتابچه فراسطوح الکترومغناطیسی*. Typography, Persian letter forms, spacing, obvious typing errors and mathematical typesetting are normalized; the prose, examples, sequence and source's simplified claims are retained. `chapter01-en.md` is the paired English translation. Separately labeled notes in the published chapter explain scientific assumptions without silently rewriting the source narrative.

The title page credits supervision to Dr. Ali Abdolali and compilation/final editing to Mohammad Salman Shahroudi. The instructor explicitly requested incorporation of the supplied text into this course. That request does not establish a general reuse license for the original booklet or third-party illustrations. Original rights and attribution remain with the source's respective creators; these transcribed source files and their translations are not an assertion that the original booklet is newly licensed under the repository's general content license. Consult the source rights holder for reuse beyond the requested course incorporation.

The PDF itself and its external raster illustrations are not redistributed. Original book-owned schematics and LaTeX descriptions provide the conceptual visual connections.

`chapter01-coverage.json` identifies the PDF by SHA-256 and records insertion coverage. It is not an automatic proof that PDF text extraction was correct. Source correspondence was reviewed from the extracted text and rendered pages.
