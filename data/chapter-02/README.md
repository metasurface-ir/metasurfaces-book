# Chapter 2 exact planar evidence

Generated by the executed `notebooks/02-waves-and-interfaces.ipynb` with
`simulations/waves_interfaces.py`. The JSON retains parameters, complex
coefficients, spectra, environment, source SHA-256, and time/basis/phase-plane
conventions. The CSV exports interface-angle, film-wavelength, and sheet-strength
sweeps. Undefined transmission phase is JSON null and CSV blank.

The JSON also retains `retrieval_conditioning`: the declared complex perturbation,
nominal and perturbed coefficients, inferred sheet parameters, exact parameter
errors, local derivative estimates, and relative perturbation magnitudes. This
is a deterministic model-consistent sensitivity experiment. It does not specify
a measurement-noise distribution; independently perturbed coefficients can fail
the continuity test and require a separate uncertainty/model treatment.

Bulk indices are positive real constants in isotropic nonmagnetic media.
The interface permits TE/TM oblique incidence, transmitted cutoff, and decaying
evanescent fields. The film and electric sheet use normal incidence. The sheet
has nonnegative conductance and may absorb; the film bulk is lossless. Jones
vectors describe ideal deterministic fully polarized fields and linear analyzers.
The Stokes V coordinate definition is explicit; no unqualified handedness label
is inferred. These are exact planar models, with no mesh or patterned geometry.

Fresnel amplitudes use a COMMON tangential E axis, including reflected TM. That
TM sign differs from a convention with a rotating local p basis. Normal power
uses the real outgoing admittance factor. TIR can have a nonzero transmitted
field while its normal transmitted power is zero. At exact TM cutoff the
tangential transmitted E vanishes while its longitudinal component need not.

Interface and sheet coefficients share a coincident reference plane. Film r is
at its entrance and t at its exit. Electric-sheet retrieval tests continuity and
transmission magnitude; it is not geometry/material retrieval. The coherent-sheet
example holds total incident power fixed at one when switching two-sided phase.
