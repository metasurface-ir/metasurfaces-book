Equations, signs, and limits

Use exp(−iωt), spatial exp(ik·r), incidence from z < 0, and periodicity along x. With qᵢ = nᵢ sin θᵢ, the tangential coordinate is qₘ = qᵢ + mλ₀/Λ. An order is open if |qₘ| < n; equality is grazing, and larger magnitude is evanescent.

The displayed normal root is κ/k₀ = √(n² − qₘ²) in the outward coordinate. In laboratory coordinates kz = +κ for transmission and −κ for reflection. Closed outgoing fields decay in both half-spaces. The dimensionless cutoff tolerance is 10⁻¹⁰ on |qₘ/n| − 1. Closed channels have no real far-field angle.

All possibly open orders are enumerated automatically, with two closed neighbors beyond conservative bounds. Indices are positive and real; the model has kᵧ = 0. It does not classify lossy/anisotropic media or calculate a metasurface's amplitudes, absorption, or efficiency.
